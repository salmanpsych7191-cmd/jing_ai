Restaurant Retail AI Agent

An AI-powered WhatsApp concierge for restaurants and retail

👤 Your Name 📅 July 2026 🎓 Computer Science

Problem Statement

Restaurants lose bookings & revenue to manual front-desk work

  • Staff miss phone calls and WhatsApp messages during peak hours.
  • Reservations are tracked on paper or spreadsheets, causing double-bookings.
  • Follow-ups (reminders, review requests, loyalty updates) are forgotten.
  • Customer data is scattered, so personalized service is nearly impossible.
  • Small restaurants cannot afford 24/7 human reception.

Target Customer

Who is this built for?

Primary: Independent Restaurants

  • Family-owned cafés and biryani houses
  • Fine-dining restaurants with no full-time reservation team
  • Cloud kitchens that take orders on WhatsApp

Secondary: Retail & Quick-Service

  • Bakeries and sweet shops with repeat customers
  • Small retail brands needing repurchase reminders
  • Any business that wants automated customer follow-up

Your AI Solution

A 24/7 AI concierge on WhatsApp

The Restaurant Retail AI Agent handles reservations, reminders, reviews, and loyalty — all through a single WhatsApp number and a staff dashboard.

How it works

  • Guest messages the restaurant on WhatsApp.
  • Groq LLM understands intent and guides the conversation.
  • Booking is saved to a SQLite database.
  • Twilio sends confirmations, reminders, and review links.
  • Staff manage everything from a web dashboard.
Architecture: WhatsApp → Twilio → FastAPI → Groq + SQLite
